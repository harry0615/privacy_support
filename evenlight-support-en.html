Getting started

Open Tonight, choose how you are arriving, and select about 1 or 3 minutes. Begin the evening reflection, read at your own pace, and optionally save a gift, a concern, or one small action for tomorrow. You can use reflections and your journal without enabling app blocking.

Which features are free?

Daily reflections, personal notes, next-evening follow-ups, app blocking, up to three routines with custom days, and optional local reminders are free. Reading, exporting, and deleting saved notes do not require Plus.

How do I set up app blocking?

Open Routine, allow Screen Time access, and choose the apps, categories, or websites you want to set aside. Choose a start time, duration, and days, enable scheduled blocking, then save. App blocking requires a supported physical iPhone and your permission. You can also start a reflection with 20 minutes of quiet after choosing your apps.

Why is blocking not starting or ending as expected?

Check that access is allowed, at least one item is selected, and your routine is enabled for the current day. Times follow your iPhone's local clock. Another overlapping routine may still be active. Open Evenlight to refresh its state; to stop, use End quiet time in Tonight or Routine. If you need to remove access, review Evenlight's Screen Time access in iPhone Settings. iOS controls the application and removal of restrictions.

Why am I not receiving reminders?

Check the reminder switch in Routine and notification permission in iPhone Settings. Focus modes and notification settings may silence or delay how a reminder is shown. Reminders do not contain your journal text.

What does Plus include?

Evenlight Plus adds two guided journeys of 14 evenings each, weekly review creation from your own saved records, and journal search. It is a one-time, non-consumable purchase with permanent Plus access and no recurring payments. The App Store displays the price before purchase.

I bought Plus. How do I restore it?

Use the same Apple Account that made the purchase, connect to the internet, then open Settings in Evenlight and select Restore purchases. Restore purchases is also available on the Plus screen. If the purchase is pending, wait for Apple to complete it. If the product cannot be loaded, try again later and contact us if it persists. Do not purchase again just to troubleshoot.

Apple handles payments and refund requests. For a refund request, visit Apple's Report a Problem. Apple determines eligibility. Restoring Plus does not restore journal entries.

Where are my notes? Can I export or delete them?

Your notes are stored on your device; there is no Evenlight account or app-managed cloud sync. In Settings, choose Export my journal to send a copy to a destination you select. Delete an individual note in Journal, or use Erase local data in Settings to remove local notes, drafts, progress, routines, and app selections. Export what you want to keep first.

Deleting a reflection also removes saved weekly reviews covering that date. Copies already exported or stored in a backup are not deleted by this action. Erasing local data does not cancel your App Store purchase. We cannot retrieve a local-only journal from an Evenlight server.

Can I use English and Korean?

Yes. Evenlight supports English and Korean and follows your iPhone's language preferences. If an app-specific language option appears in iPhone Settings, you can use it. Close and reopen the app after changing language if needed.

How is my information handled?

See the privacy policy for local storage, optional permissions, purchases, exports, backups, and support messages. Support is for app questions; Evenlight does not provide live counseling.
