Privacy Policy

Front-facing dog portraits. Let us catch the moment.

Effective September 15, 2026

Service and operator

DographyShot analyzes camera-facing dog portraits on your device and helps select clear photos. The operator is Imaezings and the developer is Hyunseung Lee. No registration or app-specific account is required.

Information processed on your device

Camera preview frames and capture candidates are analyzed on your device to assess dog presence, face direction, clarity and framing. The app stores captured photos, capture times, selected notes, voice recordings, sound preferences, free capture usage, purchase entitlements and purchase identifiers needed for restoration in local app storage. Photos and recordings are not uploaded to a developer-operated server or collected for the developer’s model training.

Permissions and their purposes

Camera: used for the preview and captures initiated by the user. Microphone: requested only when you choose the Pro voice-recording feature; built-in sounds and ordinary photo capture do not require microphone access. Add to Photos: used when you choose to save a photo or video to Photos. The app does not request read access to analyze your entire photo library. You can change permissions in iOS Settings; denying a permission limits the corresponding feature.

Advertising, tracking and external processing

The app does not include advertising, advertising-identifier tracking or behavioral analytics SDKs. Image analysis takes place on the device. App Store purchases, restores and iOS services are handled by Apple under Apple’s policies. When you share a photo to another app, the service you choose handles it under its own privacy policy.

Saving, sharing and backups

Eligible capture results may be kept in the app’s recent capture history. Saving to Photos creates a separate copy. Completing an action in the share sheet sends content to the app or recipient you choose. Copies or app data may be backed up according to your iCloud Photos, iCloud Backup or computer backup settings. The app does not operate a separate photo-sync server.

In-app purchases

DographyShot Pro is a one-time, non-consumable lifetime unlock. Apple handles payment, refunds and its purchase records. The app may store product and transaction information on your device to verify your entitlement, but does not receive or store payment card numbers or your Apple Account password.

Information you provide for support

If you email us, we receive your email address, display name, message and any materials you choose to attach. We use this information to respond and resolve the issue, not for advertising or model training. We keep it for the time needed to handle your request and necessary follow-up, then delete it, except to the extent retention is legally required. Your chosen email service and our receiving email service are involved in sending and storing messages. Please avoid sending unnecessary original photos, recordings or payment information.

Deletion and privacy requests

Delete locally stored photos from the recent capture screen and recordings from My voice. Deleting the app removes its local app data; offloading the app is not the same as deleting its data. Copies in Photos, Files, external services and backups must be managed separately. To request access, correction or deletion of personal information in support messages, contact us below. The developer cannot remotely access photos or recordings stored only on your device.

Website access and policy updates

These privacy and support pages do not themselves include advertising or visitor analytics scripts, or functionality that sets cookies. The hosting provider may process IP addresses and access logs when delivering the pages. We will update this page and its effective date if our practices change.
