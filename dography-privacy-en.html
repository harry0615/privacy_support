Dography Privacy Policy

Dography is an iPhone app that finds recent photos of your dog in your photo library and turns the week’s moments into a photo and a vertical video. Photo searches and story creation begin when you choose to start them.

Effective: November 3, 2026 · First effective: July 25, 2026 · Dography 1.7

Photos, dog profiles, identification features and recorded voice clips are processed on your device. Anonymous usage statistics sent to improve the app, and how to turn them off, are described separately in section 5.

1. Information processed on device

Dog profiles, captured or selected photos, on-device dog-presence, identity-similarity, sharpness, exposure and composition scores, plus album, card and Weekly Story data are processed in app-only storage. They are not linked to an account or sent to a Dography server.

2. Camera and Photos

The camera is used only when you choose to take a photo. After you start Weekly Story search, Dography examines recent photos within the access granted by iOS. Dog detection, comparison with reference photos, and quality ranking use Vision on device. Temporary candidate copies are cleaned up; only photo copies and final output for a story you archive remain in app-only storage.

The microphone is used only when you record “My voice” on the camera screen. The clip is stored only in the app's private storage to play back for your dog and is replaced when you record again. Reminders are scheduled on this device only when you turn them on and never pass through a server.

3. Saving, exporting, and sharing

Saving to Photos, exporting Photo Cards or ZIP backups, and opening the iOS share sheet happen only when you choose them. Copies exported to Photos, Files, or another app can remain outside Dography's app-only storage and are then governed by that app or service's retention and deletion policies.

Full Backup & Restore

Keep and restore photos, profiles, reference photos, weekly stories and card projects in a ZIP. Purchases are not included.

Restoring replaces current photos, profiles, stories and card projects with this backup. Back up your current memories first if you want to keep them. Your purchase status is preserved.

Backup ZIPs contain personal records such as photos and profiles and are not password-protected by the app. Choose trusted storage locations and recipients. Purchases and “My voice” recordings are not included. Also save finished videos you want to keep to Photos or Files.

4. Payment information

Pro purchases and restoration are handled by Apple's App Store. Dography does not directly collect card numbers, payment accounts, or Apple IDs. The app stores only the verified purchase result and Pro status. Apple's privacy policy applies to payment information handled by Apple.

To prevent repeated free trials, the trial start time is also stored in the device’s Keychain. This record may remain after deleting and reinstalling the app. Apple manages purchase records for paid and trial products.

Apple’s Privacy Policy

5. Advertising and third parties

This version of Dography contains no advertising SDK. It does not collect accounts or contacts and does not share dog photos, profiles, recorded voice clips or on-device analysis results with third parties.

Anonymous usage statistics: to improve the app, Dography may send purchase-related events (paywall views, purchase attempts and results, purchase restores, trial activity and Pro music selection), Weekly Story usage events (photo search, story completion, photos removed, whether a story was saved or shared, and numbers such as photos reviewed, processing time and error types) and a once-a-day app-use signal to TelemetryDeck GmbH (Germany). The event time, app version, platform (iOS), language/region setting, access plan (free, trial or Lifetime Pro), number of stories completed, days since install and a hash of a random install ID created on this device are included; photos, dog names, profiles and advertising identifiers are never sent. These statistics are not used to identify you or for ad tracking. Turn off “Share anonymous usage statistics” in My dog › App Information to stop sending and delete records not yet sent.

6. Retention and deletion

Profiles, album photos, dog references, selected-photo copies, and final archived stories remain on device until you delete them. Therefore an archived story remains intact if its Photos original is deleted. Deleting the app removes app-only data. Copies exported to Photos, Files, ZIP backup, or other apps must be deleted there. This version does not use automatic iCloud sync.

Deleting photos, profiles or stories from the app does not delete exported backup ZIPs. Delete those separately where they are stored. A new “My voice” recording replaces the previous clip; deleting the app removes the clip from its private storage. The trial-start record is subject to the exception in section 4.

7. Permissions and withdrawal

You can change or revoke Camera and Photos access in iOS Settings at any time. With Limited Photos access, Dography searches only allowed photos. Revoking access stops new searches but does not delete stories or profiles already archived in the app. Use the email below for privacy questions or deletion support.

Microphone and notification permissions can also be changed or withdrawn in iOS Settings. Photo search and weekly story creation remain available without microphone permission.
