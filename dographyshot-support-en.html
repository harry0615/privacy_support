Front-facing dog portraits. Let us catch the moment.

Updated September 15, 2026

How do I take a front-facing portrait?

Tap Take a front-facing photo and allow camera access. Frame your dog at eye level and choose an attention sound from the top sound menu. Tap the shutter to play the sound once and look for camera-facing portrait candidates. Sound-assisted capture selects up to 3 eligible photos; silent capture selects one.

Why was no photo saved after I tapped the shutter?

The app may not save a photo if no dog is visible, only a side or rear view is available, the eyes or nose are obscured, or the image is too dark or blurry. Try brighter light and leave enough space to show both eyes and the nose. If a front-facing portrait cannot be verified, the app does not substitute an unrelated photo. This feature estimates face direction; it does not perfectly measure pupil direction.

Why does the app say the attention sound is quiet?

With the camera screen open, raise media volume using the side buttons. The low-volume notice clears at 20% media volume or higher. If audio is routed to headphones or Bluetooth, check the output device or disconnect it. Ringer and media volume can differ. Stop the sound and use silent capture if your dog is startled.

Can I record myself calling my dog’s name?

With Pro, open the top sound menu, choose My voice, then record. Microphone access is requested when you initiate recording. Recordings stay on your device and can be previewed, selected, replaced or deleted. Built-in sounds do not require microphone permission.

Where can I purchase Pro?

Tap the current sound name and ▾ at the top of the camera screen. In My voice, tap Record a name call with Pro to open the purchase screen. You do not need to capture a photo or use up free captures first. DographyShot Pro is a one-time lifetime unlock, not an auto-renewing subscription. The purchase screen shows the App Store price for your region.

What is free, and what does Pro include?

Free use includes 3 sound-assisted capture sessions per day, with up to 3 eligible front-facing photos selected per session. Pro removes the sound-assisted capture limit and adds name-call recordings and watermark-free, high-quality sharing cards. Saving original photos to Photos is free.

How do I restore a purchase or request a refund?

Tap Restore Purchase on the purchase screen using the Apple Account that made the original purchase. Restoring an entitlement is separate from restoring photos or recordings. An internet connection may be needed for restoration. Request refunds through Apple’s Report a Problem page. The developer does not receive payment card details or issue refunds directly.

How do I save, share or delete photos?

Choose Save in the capture results to create a copy in Photos. To share, complete your chosen action in the system share sheet. Delete locally stored photos from the recent capture screen. Copies saved or shared elsewhere must be deleted from the corresponding app or service.

What if the camera or saving does not work?

Check DographyShot’s camera and add-to-Photos permissions in iOS Settings. Check microphone access if voice recording is affected. Reopen the app and make sure storage is available. Before deleting the app, export photos you want to keep to Photos or Files. Restoring Pro alone does not restore photos or recordings on another device.

What should I include in a support request?

Email your iPhone model, iOS version, DographyShot version, the affected screen, steps to reproduce the issue and any error message. If needed, attach a screenshot with personal information hidden. Never send passwords or card numbers. Provide original photos or audio only voluntarily when needed to investigate the issue.

Keep your dog comfortable

Do not force a pose or play loud sounds repeatedly. Stop and take a break if your dog is startled, turns away or appears tired. You do not need to keep trying to obtain a successful capture.
