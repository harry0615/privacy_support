ToyToStory · A guide for caregivers

Privacy Policy

Read, choose, and play with the friend in your photo.

Effective October 27, 2026

Photos, stories, recordings, and child profiles are processed on your device and are not sent to a developer server.

Apple purchases, the free trial and reviews, system services such as Siri, notifications and image generation, files a caregiver chooses to export, and support or website visits are explained separately below.

The app and its operator

ToyToStory turns toys and drawings into story characters, offers branching endings, helps caregivers continue the experience through play, and reads stories at bedtime in a caregiver’s voice. It is designed with children ages 4–7 using the app together with a caregiver in mind. The operator is Imaezings and the developer is Hyunseung Lee. No ToyToStory account or registration is required.

Information processed on your device

The app stores the following on this device or processes it on the device:

  • Friends (characters): selected or captured photos, character cutouts, toy names and descriptions
  • Child profiles (up to 3, optional): a name or nickname (up to 12 characters), an age setting (4–7), a story language, and up to three “favorite things” chosen from a fixed list. No date of birth is requested.
  • Stories and progress: stories and ending choices, Korean and English versions, bookshelf and favorites, reading and play progress, selected feelings, and progress in continuing stories
  • Voices: caregiver voice recordings by scene (up to two people), voices received from family, and “family greeting” recordings
  • Cover pictures (experimental): story cover images generated on the device
  • Settings: reading speed, AI preference, night screen, bedtime listening (sound, length, nightly story limit), bedtime reminder time, and whether to use Apple Personal Voice
  • Records for the free trial and review requests: described under “Purchases, free trial, and review requests” below.

This content is not automatically uploaded to a developer-operated server or collected to train the developer’s AI. Answers to caregiver checks are used for the check and are not transmitted externally. The app has no public community or user-to-user messaging.

Permissions

  • Camera: requested when you choose to photograph a toy or drawing.
  • Photo selection: Apple’s system picker supplies only the image you select. The app does not request access to your entire photo library.
  • Microphone: requested when you start a caregiver voice recording or a family greeting recording. Reading stories and system narration do not require it.
  • Add to Photos (add only): requested when a caregiver saves a playtime memory card to Photos. The app does not read your existing photos.
  • Notifications: requested when a caregiver turns on the bedtime reminder.
  • Apple Personal Voice: requested when a caregiver chooses to hear English stories in their Personal Voice. The app never receives the recordings used to create a Personal Voice.

Images are resized for use in the app (at most 1536 pixels on the long side), and saved copies are processed without retaining location or other original metadata. Faces, addresses, names, and other information visible within the image are not automatically obscured. Prefer photos of toys or drawings and avoid entering real names or contact details. Permissions can be changed in iOS Settings. You can also begin with a name without a photo, or read without recording.

On-device AI, image generation, and voices

Story generation and translation: On supported devices, operating systems, languages, and settings, Apple’s on-device model (Foundation Models) can generate new stories or translate a story into the other language. Depending on the supported environment, it uses the photo itself or image-description hints obtained on the device, and a child profile’s “favorite things” as small story hints. Requests are not sent to a developer-operated server or a separate cloud AI service. Ready-made stories remain available if AI is unavailable.

Caregiver review: AI-written stories and translations are shown to the child and read aloud only after a caregiver reads and approves them. Ready-made stories written by the developer open directly; caregivers can choose to review those first too in caregiver settings. This step does not guarantee that AI text is accurate or appropriate in every situation.

Cover pictures (experimental): On supported devices (Image Playground with Apple Intelligence, iOS 18.4 or later), a cover is drawn on the device only when a caregiver asks. Only English words such as “a red teddy bear in a forest village” (the toy’s kind and color and the story world) are given to the model, never the photo, a child’s name, or a friend’s name. Only Apple’s built-in styles are used, never an external provider’s style.

Voices: Narration uses installed system voices, and bedtime sounds such as rain are generated on the device. Retrieving an original photo from iCloud or preparing system voices, AI models, or image models may involve an internet connection to Apple services.

Siri, Shortcuts, and bedtime reminders

Siri and Shortcuts: The app offers shortcuts such as “Play bedtime stories in ToytoStory.” Apple recognizes and processes voice requests; the app only learns which shortcut ran.

Bedtime reminder: When a caregiver turns it on, a reminder at the chosen time is scheduled on this device only. No push token or server is used, and the reminder text is fixed. Turning it off removes the schedule.

Purchases, free trial, and review requests

PRO: a one-time, non-consumable purchase, not an automatically renewing subscription. It can be shared through Apple Family Sharing. Apple handles payment, purchase history, and refunds. The app uses StoreKit-verified purchase information to determine PRO access and never receives payment card numbers or Apple Account passwords.

14-day free trial: a separate free (price 0) item through the App Store, with no automatic charge. The trial period is counted from the trial start date verified by Apple. So that turning the device clock back does not extend it, the app keeps the latest time it has seen in local settings.

Review requests: To avoid frequent review requests, the app stores its first-use time, up to three distinct usage dates, up to three identifiers of stories read or played to the end, whether a first recording was made, the last request time, and app versions in which a request was attempted in local settings. These records are not sent to a developer server or analytics service. The app requests Apple’s standard rating interface only in the caregiver area after usage conditions are met. Apple controls display and submission, and the app cannot see your rating or review.

Files a caregiver chooses to export

These files are created only when a caregiver chooses to, and go to the app, place, or person the caregiver picks. The developer does not receive them (unless you attach one to a support email).

  • Bookshelf backup file (.ttstory): contains stories, photos, recordings, friends, child profiles, and continuing-story progress. It does not contain family greetings, cover pictures, or app settings. Anyone with the file can open its contents, so keep it somewhere safe.
  • Recording request and voice gift files: contain one story and its photos, plus, for a voice gift, only the recordings of the voice the caregiver chose. The recipient opens the file in ToyToStory and adds it after a caregiver check. Send them only to family you trust.
  • Playtime memory cards and picture-book PDFs: including the child’s name is off by default. You can choose whether the sharing text includes an App Store link.

Children and external services

The app does not include third-party advertising, advertising-identifier tracking, or usage-analytics SDKs. Purchases and the trial, settings, recordings, file export and import, deletion, and links to privacy and support pages are in the caregiver area behind a caregiver check (a sum). Leaving that area or reaching the authorization timeout requires a new check. Visiting a website or sending email involves the privacy practices of the browser, hosting provider, or email service. The caregiver task is an app interaction safeguard, not a substitute for any separate consent required by law.

Retention, backup, and deletion

Saved stories, photos, recordings, cover pictures, and child profiles remain on the device until you delete them or delete the app. The app’s data folder is marked to be excluded from iCloud and device backups, and the app has no developer-operated sync or backup service. When changing devices, you can move your bookshelf with the bookshelf backup file described above. Some settings, such as reading preferences, may be included in the operating system’s backup. Restoring PRO on another device does not restore its bookshelf, photos, or recordings.

In caregiver settings, you can delete individual stories, friends, child profiles, greeting recordings, and cover pictures, or clear the whole bookshelf. A photo still used by another story or friend may be retained. Clearing everything removes stories, friends, app photos and cover pictures, all recordings (including greetings), continuing-story progress, and the story identifiers used for review timing. Child profiles, reading and bedtime settings, the bedtime reminder, and review-request and trial history remain; each can be removed or turned off separately.

Deleting the app removes its local data, settings, and scheduled bedtime reminder from this device. Offloading an app is different from deleting it. Original photos in Photos, files a caregiver exported, Apple purchase and review records, and support emails are not deleted by removing the app. The developer cannot remotely access or recover information stored only on your device.

Information you send to support

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address, display name, message, and any attachments you voluntarily provide. We use this information to respond, troubleshoot, and handle necessary follow-up, not for advertising or AI training. It is deleted after the necessary support period, except where retention is required by law. Sending and receiving email involves the respective email providers. Do not send a child’s face, real name, address, unnecessary photos or audio, bookshelf backup files, passwords, or card details.

Privacy requests

Caregivers can contact us below about access, correction, deletion, or other applicable requests concerning information included in support communications. Manage on-device information directly through caregiver settings. Requests about purchases, the free trial, or reviews handled by Apple may also need to be made to Apple.
