ToyToStory · A guide for caregivers

Privacy Policy

Read, choose, and play with the friend in your photo.

Effective September 19, 2026

Photos, stories, and recordings are processed on your device.

Apple purchases, reviews, system services, and caregiver-initiated support or website visits are explained separately below.

The app and its operator

ToyToStory turns toys and drawings into story characters, offers branching endings, and helps caregivers continue the experience through play. It is designed with children ages 4–7 using the app together with a caregiver in mind. The operator is Imaezings and the developer is Hyunseung Lee. No ToyToStory account or registration is required.

Information processed on your device

The app stores or processes selected or captured photos, character cutouts, toy names and descriptions, the selected age setting (4–7), stories and ending choices, bookshelf and toy lists, favorites, reading and play progress, selected feelings, caregiver voice recordings, reading speed, and AI preferences. The age setting does not ask for a date of birth.

This content is not automatically uploaded to a developer-operated server or collected to train the developer’s AI. Answers to caregiver checks are used for the check and are not transmitted externally. The app has no public community or user-to-user messaging.

Photo, camera, and microphone access

  • Camera: requested when you choose to photograph a toy or drawing.
  • Photo selection: Apple’s system picker supplies the image you select. The app does not request access to your entire photo library.
  • Microphone: requested when you start a PRO caregiver voice recording. Reading stories and using system narration do not require microphone access.

Images are resized for use in the app, and saved copies are processed without retaining the original location metadata. Faces, addresses, names, and other information visible within the image are not automatically obscured. Prefer photos of toys or drawings and avoid entering real names or contact details. Permissions can be changed in iOS Settings. You can also begin with a character name without a photo, or read without recording.

On-device AI and narration

On supported devices, operating systems, languages, and settings, Apple’s on-device model can generate new stories. Depending on the supported environment, the app uses the photo itself or image-description hints obtained on the device. Generation requests are not sent to a developer-operated server or a separate cloud AI service. Ready-made stories remain available if AI is unavailable or generation fails.

A caregiver reviews the character photo, story, and both endings before a new story is shown to the child. This step does not guarantee that AI text is accurate or appropriate in every situation. Narration uses installed system voices. Retrieving an original photo from iCloud or preparing system voices or AI models may involve an internet connection to Apple services.

Purchases and App Store review requests

ToyToStory PRO is a one-time, non-consumable purchase, not an automatically renewing subscription. Apple handles payment, purchase history, and refunds. The app uses StoreKit-verified entitlements to determine PRO access. It does not receive payment card numbers or Apple Account passwords.

To avoid frequent review requests, the app stores its first-use time, up to three distinct usage dates, up to three completed-story identifiers, the last request time, and app versions in which a request was attempted in local settings. These records are not sent to a developer server or analytics service. The app requests Apple’s standard rating interface only in the caregiver area after usage conditions are met. Apple controls display and submission. The app cannot determine your rating or read your submitted review.

Children and external services

The app does not include third-party advertising, advertising-identifier tracking, or usage-analytics SDKs. Purchases, settings, and links to privacy and support pages are in the caregiver area. Leaving that area or reaching the authorization timeout requires a new check. Visiting a website or sending email involves the privacy practices of the browser, hosting provider, or email service. The caregiver task is an app interaction safeguard, not a substitute for any separate consent required by law.

Retention, backup, and deletion

Saved stories, photos, and recordings remain locally until you delete them or delete the app. Bookshelf and media folders are marked to be excluded from backups. The app has no developer-operated sync or backup service. Backup of other settings or original photos may depend on the operating system and your separate settings. Restoring PRO on another device does not restore its bookshelf, photos, or recordings.

In caregiver settings, you can delete individual stories or saved toys, or clear the whole bookshelf. A photo still used by another story or saved toy may be retained. Clearing everything removes the bookshelf, saved toys, app photos, recordings, and completed-story identifiers used for review timing. Some settings, including reading preferences and review-request frequency history, remain.

Deleting the app removes its local app data and settings from this device. Offloading an app is different from deleting it. Original photos in Photos, separate backups, Apple purchase and review records, and support emails are not deleted by removing the app. The developer cannot remotely access or recover content stored only on your device.

Information you send to support

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address, display name, message, and any attachments you voluntarily provide. We use this information to respond, troubleshoot, and handle necessary follow-up, not for advertising or AI training. It is deleted after the necessary support period, except where retention is required by law. Sending and receiving email involves the respective email providers. Do not send a child’s face, real name, address, unnecessary photos or audio, passwords, or card details.

Privacy requests

Caregivers can contact us below about access, correction, deletion, or other applicable requests concerning information included in support communications. Manage on-device content directly through caregiver settings. Requests about purchases or reviews handled by Apple may also need to be made to Apple.

This website and policy changes

These pages do not include advertising, analytics scripts, external fonts, or code that sets cookies. Hosting providers such as GitHub Pages may process IP addresses and access logs for security and service operation. If app features or data handling change, we will update this policy and its effective date and provide any required notice.
