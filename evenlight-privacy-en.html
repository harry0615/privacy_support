Evenlight offers personal Christian evening reflection and voluntary time away from selected apps. No Evenlight account is required.

Your journal and settings

Reflection notes, feelings, reflection dates, drafts, gratitude, concerns, small actions, follow-up choices, journey progress, weekly reviews, and routine settings stay on your device. Notes and reviews are not automatically uploaded to a server or sent to an AI service. Weekly reviews are assembled locally from your own saved records.

Optional Screen Time access

Apple asks for authorization when you choose app blocking. You select apps, categories, and websites using Apple's system picker. Evenlight stores opaque selection tokens, schedule settings, and the app language in a local App Group container shared with its extensions. These are used to display and apply your chosen restrictions. Evenlight does not collect browsing content or measure actual app-usage savings.

App blocking, up to three routines, custom days, and local reminders are available without Plus. You can end quiet time, change a routine, or revoke Screen Time access in iPhone Settings. Revoking access may require selecting apps again after reauthorization. Reflections and your journal remain available without this permission.

Purchases and reminders

Apple processes payments. StoreKit verifies ownership of the non-consumable Evenlight Plus purchase, and the app stores the verified access state locally. Evenlight does not receive your payment-card details. Apple's account and transaction handling follows Apple's policies. Plus unlocks guided journeys, weekly review creation, and journal search; it does not renew automatically.

Evening reminders are local notifications and require your permission. They never contain journal text. You can turn them off in Evenlight or iPhone Settings.

Exporting and contacting support

Exporting your journal can include notes, feelings, gratitude, concerns, actions, follow-up status, and saved weekly reviews. You choose the destination using the share sheet. The destination's privacy practices apply to copies you send there.

If you email support, the operator receives the email address and information you choose to include and uses them to answer your request. You do not need to attach private journal content. Your choices and privacy requests

You can access, export, and delete your local records inside the app. Contact {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} to ask about, correct, or request deletion of information you sent to support. We may ask for limited information to verify that a request relates to you. We do not need your journal text to process a privacy inquiry.

Advertising and tracking

This app has no advertising, tracking, or third-party analytics SDK. It does not sell personal information. Opening a public policy or support website involves your browser and the website's hosting service; the handling of connection information is described in the website section above.

Retention, deletion, and backups

Local records remain until you delete them or remove the app's data. Device and computer backups may include app data according to your backup settings. Copies already exported or backed up are not erased by deleting a note inside Evenlight. Shared extension data is cleared by the app's Erase local data action.

You can read, export, and delete saved records without Plus. Deleting a reflection also deletes saved weekly reviews covering that date to remove quoted words. Erase local data removes local notes, drafts, routines, progress, and app selections and ends quiet time. Your App Store purchase is retained and can be restored with the same Apple Account.

Deletion and security

In-app deletion removes the selected records from the app's active local storage. It does not remotely erase exported copies or backups. When support information reaches the end of the retention period stated above, the operator deletes it or irreversibly anonymizes it, subject to any identified legal retention obligation. Use a device passcode and protect your Apple Account and any destination where you export notes. No storage system can be guaranteed risk-free.

Intended use and changes

Evenlight is designed for personal reflection and voluntary self-control, not as a children's or parental-control service. It does not collect a birth date or require an in-app age declaration. Optional Screen Time access uses Apple's system authorization. The operator will update this page if the app's data handling changes.
