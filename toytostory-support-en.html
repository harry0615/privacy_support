Frequently asked questions

How do we get started?

Start a new story, choose a character name, and optionally photograph a toy or drawing or select a photo. Choose a story world and age setting. A caregiver reviews the photo, story, and both endings before you read together, choose an ending, and continue with play.

When is a caregiver check needed?

Each new story requires approval because even ready-made stories can include your entered name, description, or selected photo. Previously approved saved stories can be read again. Purchases, settings, recordings, deletion, and external information pages are managed in the caregiver area. Leaving it or reaching the authorization timeout requires another check.

Why is AI unavailable or unable to make a story?

AI depends on the device, operating system, language, Apple Intelligence settings, and model readiness. Check the status in caregiver settings. Ready-made stories remain available without AI or if generation fails. Buying PRO does not change your device’s AI compatibility.

Why does the character cutout look wrong?

Try a clear, well-lit photo against a simple background. If separation fails, you can continue with the original photo. You can also choose the original-photo view. A caregiver should check that the image does not include a child’s face or contact details.

Why is narration quiet or silent?

Start narration and adjust media volume to a comfortable level using the device’s side buttons. Check whether audio is routed to Bluetooth or headphones. Ringer volume can differ from media volume. If a matching system voice is unavailable, check iOS voice settings; you can read the text together in the meantime.

How do I record my voice?

With PRO, first save the story to your bookshelf, then choose caregiver voice recording. Complete the caregiver check and allow microphone access. Recordings last up to three minutes; recordings shorter than one second are not saved. Saving a new recording replaces that story’s previous recording.

Where can I buy or restore PRO?

From Home, tap Parents, enter the answer to the displayed sum, then open PRO features, purchase and restore. The purchase screen shows your App Store price. To restore an existing purchase, use the same Apple Account and choose Restore Purchase. Pending purchases unlock after approval.

Does restoring PRO restore my stories and photos?

Purchase restoration restores PRO access only. Your bookshelf, photos, and recordings are local data with no separate sync service, so they are not restored with a purchase. Check this before deleting the app to troubleshoot. Offloading and deleting an app are different actions.

How can I delete my bookshelf or photos?

In caregiver settings, delete individual stories or toys or clear everything. A photo still used by another story or saved toy may remain. Deleting app content does not delete originals in Photos. Some settings, including reading preferences and review-request frequency history, remain after clearing the bookshelf.

Why does no rating prompt appear?

The app requests Apple’s standard review interface only in caregiver settings after usage and completed-play conditions are met. Apple and device settings determine whether it appears. Ratings and reviews are never required to use a feature.

What if the camera or microphone does not work?

Check ToyToStory’s permissions in iOS Settings and make sure storage is available. You can select a photo or start with only a name instead of using the camera. System narration does not require microphone access. If the issue continues, contact us with the affected screen and error message.

What should we keep in mind during play?

A caregiver should read the story and activity instructions first and adapt them to the child’s development and surroundings. Avoid hazards such as small parts, heights, water, fire, and sharp objects. Stop if the child is uncomfortable. Stories do not replace professional educational, developmental, or safety judgment.

Refund requests

For purchases billed by Apple, you can request a refund through Apple’s Report a Problem page. Apple determines eligibility.

What to include in your message

Include your device model, iOS version, app version, affected screen, steps to reproduce the issue, and any error message. If needed, attach a screenshot with personal information hidden. Do not send passwords, card details, children’s real names or faces, or unnecessary audio.
