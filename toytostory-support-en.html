Frequently asked questions

How do we get started?

Start a new story, choose a friend’s name, and optionally photograph a toy or drawing or select a photo. You can also start with just a name. Choose a story world and the story opens; read together, choose an ending, and continue with play. If you create a child profile, the age setting and story language are set automatically.

When is a caregiver check needed?

AI-written stories and translations appear to the child only after a caregiver reads and approves them. Ready-made stories written by the developer open directly; you can choose to review those first too in caregiver settings. Purchases, settings, recordings, reminders, file export and import, deletion, and external information pages are managed in the caregiver area. Leaving it or reaching the authorization timeout requires another check.

Why is AI unavailable or unable to make a story?

AI depends on the device, operating system, language, Apple Intelligence settings, and model readiness. Check the status in caregiver settings. Ready-made stories remain available without AI or if generation fails. Buying PRO does not change your device’s AI compatibility.

Why does the character cutout look wrong?

Try a clear, well-lit photo against a simple background. If separation fails, you can continue with the original photo or choose the original-photo view. A caregiver should check that the image does not include a child’s face or contact details.

Why is narration quiet or silent?

Start narration and adjust media volume with the device’s side buttons. Check whether audio is routed to Bluetooth or headphones. Ringer volume can differ from media volume. If a matching system voice is unavailable, check iOS voice settings; you can read the text together in the meantime.

How do I record my voice?

Save the story to your bookshelf, then choose voice recording in the caregiver area. After allowing microphone access, you record scene by scene and move on with “Save and record the next scene.” Each scene can be up to three minutes; recordings shorter than one second are not saved. Re-recording replaces only that scene. With PRO, a second person can record too. English stories can also be read in your Apple Personal Voice (Apple does not yet support Korean).

What are “Our family greetings”?

Record short greetings once, for the start of every story, between stories, and at the end of bedtime. Free includes the goodnight greeting; PRO includes all three. Greetings are stored on this device only and are not included in a bookshelf backup file.

How can grandparents send their voice?

In the caregiver area, pick a story and use “Ask someone to record” (PRO) to send a file by Messages or another app. The recipient opens it in ToyToStory, records it scene by scene for free, and sends it back with “Send my voice.” Opening the returned voice gift adds it to your story after a caregiver check (receiving requires PRO). These files contain the story, photos, and recordings, so send them only to family you trust.

How do bedtime listening, the bedtime reminder, and Siri work?

Bedtime listening reads your chosen stories one after another and ends with a calm sound and a timer. Set the order in “Choose tonight’s stories”; the nightly number of stories and the length can be changed in caregiver settings. Turn on the bedtime reminder to get a notification at the time you choose. You can also ask Siri, “Play bedtime stories in ToytoStory” or “Read today’s story in ToytoStory.”

What are child profiles and favorite things?

Add up to three children, each with a name (up to 12 characters), an age setting (4–7), and a story language. Choose up to three favorite things, such as bunnies, dinosaurs, trains, or strawberries, and new stories include them as small details. No date of birth is requested.

Can we read another story with the same friend?

When you reach the goodnight page, “Start the next adventure” opens another story starring the same friend. If your bookshelf has room, the current story is saved first. In the two free continuing series (three parts each), the next part opens once you reach goodnight. With PRO, another saved friend can join the story as “a friend to join in.”

How does the 14-day free trial work?

Start it from “Try PRO free for 14 days” in the caregiver area. It is a free App Store item with no automatic charge, available once per Apple Account. After 14 days the app returns to free features, and everything you made during the trial stays.

Where can I buy or restore PRO?

From Home, tap Parents, enter the answer to the displayed sum, then open “PRO · Purchase and restore.” The purchase screen shows your App Store price. To restore an existing purchase, use the same Apple Account and choose “Restore purchase.” PRO shared through Family Sharing is applied the same way. Pending purchases unlock after approval.

How do I move my bookshelf to a new device?

Restoring a purchase restores PRO access only. Your bookshelf, photos, and recordings are stored on the device and excluded from iCloud backup, so use “Save a bookshelf backup” on the old device and “Choose a backup file” on the new one. The backup file includes stories, photos, recordings, friends, and child profiles, but not family greetings, cover pictures, or app settings. Make a backup file before deleting the app. Offloading and deleting an app are different actions.

How can I delete my bookshelf or photos?

In caregiver settings, delete individual stories, friends, child profiles, greetings, or cover pictures, or clear everything. A photo still used by another story or friend may remain. Clearing everything removes stories, friends, photos, covers, all recordings, and continuing-story progress; child profiles, reading and bedtime settings, the bedtime reminder, and review-request and trial history remain. Deleting app content does not delete originals in Photos or files you already exported.

Why don’t I see “Cover picture (experiment)”?

It appears only on devices that support Image Playground with Apple Intelligence (iOS 18.4 or later). The cover is drawn on the device from words only — the toy’s kind, color, and story world — never your photo or names. It is experimental, so you can redraw or delete a cover you don’t like.

The text is too small, or screen motion is too much.

The app follows the iOS text size setting, up to 1.4×. Turning on Reduce Motion in iOS, or the night screen in caregiver settings, stops the stage motion.

Why does no rating prompt appear?

The app requests Apple’s standard review interface only on a caregiver screen after a few days of use and stories enjoyed to the end. Apple and device settings determine whether it appears. Ratings and reviews are never required to use a feature.

What if the camera, microphone, or reminders do not work?

Check ToyToStory’s permissions in iOS Settings and make sure storage is available. You can select a photo or start with only a name instead of using the camera, and system narration works without the microphone. If bedtime reminders don’t arrive, check notification permission and Focus settings in iOS. If the issue continues, contact us with the affected screen and error message.

What should we keep in mind during play?

A caregiver should read the story and activity instructions first and adapt them to the child’s development and surroundings. Avoid hazards such as small parts, heights, water, fire, and sharp objects. Stop if the child is uncomfortable. Stories do not replace professional educational, developmental, or safety judgment.

Refund requests

For purchases billed by Apple, you can request a refund through Apple’s Report a Problem page. Apple determines eligibility.

What to include in your message

Include your device model, iOS version, app version, affected screen, steps to reproduce the issue, and any error message. If needed, attach a screenshot with personal information hidden. Do not send passwords, card details, children’s real names or faces, unnecessary audio, or bookshelf backup files.
