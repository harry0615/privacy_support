Dography Support

Dography is an iPhone app that finds recent photos of your dog in your photo library and turns the week’s moments into a photo and a vertical video. Photo searches and story creation begin when you choose to start them.

What to include when contacting us

  • Your iPhone model and iOS version
  • Your Dography version and selected app language
  • The screen, steps to reproduce the issue and any error message

Do not send passwords or payment details. Original photos and backup ZIPs contain personal records, so there is no need to attach them unless they are needed to investigate the issue.

Frequently asked questions

How do I make a weekly story?

Start Find my dog in This week to search recent photos allowed by your Photos permission and select good shots on your device. Your first story uses the last 30 days; later stories use the past week. Review the picks and finish the story to create a photo result. With 3–5 photos, you can also make a 12-, 16- or 20-second vertical video.

Can I choose photos myself or stop a search?

Choose Photos lets you select up to 5 photos. You can also stop a search and switch to manual selection. A weekly video needs 3–5 different photos. If an iCloud-only photo cannot be read, download its original in Photos and select it again.

Another dog appears, or there are too few results.

Remove a wrong pick with ×, or use Not mine if it is another dog. Confirmed photos help future recognition. Results are not always accurate, so review them before finishing. You can choose the photos you want manually.

Photo search, capture or saving is not working.

Check Photos and Camera permissions for Dography in iOS Settings. Limited Photos access searches only allowed photos; use Choose Photos to select others. Saving to Photos also needs permission to add photos. If storage is low, free some space and try again.

How do My voice and auto burst work in the camera?

On the camera screen, hold 🎙 to record your dog’s name or another sound for about 3 seconds. Microphone permission is requested only for recording, and the clip stays on the device. With auto burst enabled, playing a sound takes 3 photos and picks a good one. Photos are not captured automatically outside the camera screen.

How do I save a story or share it with music?

Choose the photo or video on the finished story screen, then Save or Share. You can choose background music or export without music. If video creation is incomplete or music mixing fails, share the photo or try again. Copies exported to Photos or another app are kept separately from Dography.

What is free, and what does Pro add?

You can make free 720p weekly videos with the Dography mark and use 3 basic background tracks. Lifetime Pro unlocks every background track, future tracks and 1080p weekly videos without the Dography mark. This version has no ads. Check the price on the app’s App Store purchase screen.

Will the 7-day free trial charge me automatically?

No. Pro features are available during the trial, and photos and videos you make remain yours after it ends. To keep making videos with Pro music and mark-free 1080p output, make a separate one-time Lifetime Pro purchase. Reinstalling the app does not restart the trial.

How do I restore purchases?

Use the Apple Account that made the purchase and select Restore Purchases on the purchase screen. Purchase records are not included in a full-backup ZIP, and restoring a backup does not change your current purchase status.

What should I do before changing devices or deleting the app?

Open Full Backup & Restore in My Dog, create a full backup and save the ZIP to Files or another safe location. On the destination device, choose a Dography full-backup ZIP. Restoring replaces current photos, profiles, stories and existing card projects, so back up current memories first. There is no automatic iCloud sync. My voice recordings and purchase records are not included in the ZIP.

Does deleting an original photo remove my finished story?

An archived story keeps copies of its selected photos, so deleting the Photos original does not remove that story. Deleting a story in Dography removes its output and its copied source photos. Copies exported to Photos, Files, backup ZIPs or other apps must be deleted separately there.

When do weekly reminders arrive, and how do I turn them off?

If enabled, a reminder arrives on Sunday at 7 PM to help you make another story. The reminder is skipped if you have already made that week’s story. Turn reminders off in My Dog or change notification permission in iOS Settings. Reminders do not schedule background photo searches or story generation.

Which languages are supported?

Korean, English, Japanese, Spanish and French are supported. Dography follows the app language selected in iOS. Change it in Dography’s language settings in iOS; if the option is missing, check your preferred iOS languages. Date text follows the app language, while the default kg/lb unit keeps your device region setting.

How do I stop anonymous usage statistics?

Turn off Share anonymous usage statistics in My Dog › App Information to stop sending and delete records not yet sent. Photos, dog names, profiles and voice clips are not included in those statistics. Section 5 of the privacy policy describes the data and provider.

Keep photography comfortable

Stop taking photos or playing sounds if your dog seems uncomfortable or tired. Voice recording is optional; searching photos for a weekly story does not require microphone access.
